The release format is JSONL-first with a CSV convenience export. Each evaluation carries a dataset version, creation timestamp, category, language, fingerprint, preference, quality scores, and rationale.

Current status: MVP research release. Sample or locally collected records must not be marketed as a production-scale human preference dataset. Confirm licensing and permissions for every source before commercial distribution.
