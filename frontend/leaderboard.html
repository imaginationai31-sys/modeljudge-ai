Benchmark interpretation

Reviewer score is the average of the eight stored quality dimensions across submitted reviews.

Review coverage is reviews divided by evaluations and can exceed one when evaluations receive multiple reviews.

Tie rate is descriptive only; it should not be used alone to rank reviewer quality.

Leaderboard methodology

The current leaderboard ranks reviewers by average quality score, then review count. This is an MVP analytics view. Future versions should add gold-task calibration, agreement-adjusted quality, confidence intervals, and reviewer eligibility rules before using rankings for operational decisions.
