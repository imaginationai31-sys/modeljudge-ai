DUE DILIGENCE

What a buyer should verify.

Before a commercial acquisition or deployment, independently verify repository ownership, third-party dependencies, production infrastructure, security configuration, scalability, dataset provenance, privacy/PII considerations, applicable licenses, and commercial rights. Software ownership and dataset rights should be documented separately where required.
